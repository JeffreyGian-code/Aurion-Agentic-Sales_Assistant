# Vehicle catalogue knowledge

This guide covers 200 seeded vehicle variants across 20 models. Each section gives a shared model description, equipment differences from Indian manufacturer sources, and the exact catalogue IDs and trim labels. Listed prices in INR come from [the inventory seed](../../../database/seed.sql).

**How to read trim comparisons.** The seed records a trim label and price but gives no model year, powertrain or equipment list. A shared label is a reference for comparison. Equipment for a seeded ID remains unverified until the vehicle's year and configuration are matched to the manufacturer source.

## Honda Amaze
